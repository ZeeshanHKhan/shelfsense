# Store SOP 4.2 - Shelf Price Label Compliance

1. The system price in the POS is always the source of truth. Never change the POS price from the shelf.
2. If the shelf label is HIGHER than the system price: replace the label within 4 hours. The customer is charged the lower system price at checkout, so this is a customer trust issue, not a revenue issue.
3. If the shelf label is LOWER than the system price: replace the label within 1 hour. State pricing laws may require honoring the lower shelf price at checkout, so this is a revenue and compliance risk.
4. Print replacement labels from the handheld using the "Label Reprint" workflow and the aisle printer.
5. If the barcode is not found in the system, set the item aside and notify the department manager. Do not sell unknown items.
6. After replacing a label, rescan it to close the exception.
